# Products, buying decisions, compatibility, and recalls

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manufacturer manuals and support | Specifications, compatibility, warranty, and firmware | Search exact model number, revision, and region. Confirm manuals and support dates against the product label. |
| [CPSC recalls](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls) | US consumer-product recalls | Search brand and model. Match the serial range, manufacture date, or batch before drawing conclusions. |
| Official retailer or service terms | Current price, stock, plan inclusions, and return terms | Check the exact regional listing, seller, and variant. Include fees and shipping when comparing totals. |

Use independent tests for measured performance. Compare the same configuration and distinguish advertised specifications from observed results.
