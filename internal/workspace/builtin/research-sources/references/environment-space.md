# Climate, weather, wildlife, Earth science, and space

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [NOAA NCEI](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/) | Historical weather and climate observations | Search dataset, station, and date range. Check units, coverage, and quality flags. |
| [USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/) | Earthquakes, geology, water, and US geospatial data | Search the event ID, site ID, place, or dataset. Note sampling method, time zone, and update date. |
| [GBIF](https://www.gbif.org/) | Species names and biodiversity occurrence records | Search the scientific name and region, then filter dates and inspect the source dataset and coordinate uncertainty. |
| [NASA Science](https://science.nasa.gov/) | Space missions, astronomy, and NASA science data | Search mission, instrument, or object name. Follow mission pages to data products and publications. |

Distinguish observations, projections, and live reports. A missing occurrence record alone gives little evidence about absence.
