# Law, regulation, courts, and public policy

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official legislature, gazette, regulator, or court | Primary legal texts and authoritative decisions in the relevant jurisdiction | Search the jurisdiction and exact citation, case number, or instrument title. Open the issuing body's record. |
| [GovInfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/) | US federal statutes, regulations, and official publications | Search citation, public law number, or exact title. Compare the published text with later amendments. |
| [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/) | EU legislation, consolidated texts, and case law | Search by CELEX number, instrument number, or case number. Check applicability, consolidation date, and national implementation where relevant. |

Identify the jurisdiction and date first. Distinguish proposals, enacted rules, effective rules, and enforcement guidance.
