# Food, cooking, nutrition, and recalls

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/) | Nutrient composition and serving weights | Search the ingredient and preparation state. Compare the same weight basis and check branded versus generic entries. |
| [FoodSafety.gov recalls and outbreaks](https://www.foodsafety.gov/recalls-and-outbreaks) | US food recall notices and outbreak links | Search product and brand. Confirm the lot, region, and date on the issuing agency's notice. |
| Original recipe publisher or test kitchen | Techniques, quantities, and tested substitutions | Search dish and technique. Check weights, oven settings, and whether a substitution was actually tested. |

For substitutions, distinguish culinary effects from allergy or food-safety implications.
