# Language, usage, translation, and etymology

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/) | English meanings, usage notes, and pronunciation | Search the headword, then compare the sense and examples with the sentence at hand. |
| [CNRTL](https://www.cnrtl.fr/definition/) and [RAE dictionary](https://dle.rae.es/) | French and Spanish lexical references | Search the infinitive or dictionary form. Check regional, historical, and register labels. |
| Original-language sources and parallel texts | Idiom, terminology, and translation in context | Search the exact phrase and shorter variants. Compare like genres and regions, and check an official translation if the wording is institutional. |

For etymology, distinguish an attested earlier form from a proposed origin.
