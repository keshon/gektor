# News, current events, and claim verification

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Issuing agency, election authority, court, or organization | Original statements, votes, orders, decisions, and datasets | Search names and event date on the issuing body's site. Open the statement, filing, dataset, or recording. |
| Local and independent reporting | On-the-ground coverage and corroboration | Search location, date, and named entities in relevant languages. Check the byline, update time, sources, and corrections. |
| [Internet Archive Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/) | Historical captures of specific web pages | Start with the original URL and choose a capture near the relevant date. Check for missing media and redirects. |

Distinguish event date from publication date. Compare independent reporting and label claims that remain disputed or unverified.
