# Science, papers, experiments, and patents

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Crossref](https://search.crossref.org/) | Publication metadata and DOI lookup | Search the exact paper title or DOI. Confirm authors, venue, and year before following the publisher link. |
| [PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) | Full text of deposited biomedical papers | Search title or DOI. Check whether the full text is a published paper or an author manuscript. |
| [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/search/) | Preprints in physics, mathematics, and computing | Search title or topic, then check version history and any linked journal publication. |
| [WIPO PATENTSCOPE](https://patentscope.wipo.int/) | Published patent applications | Search publication number first, then applicant and title. Verify legal status with the issuing office. |

Use publication metadata to identify a work. Check the paper itself for methods, limitations, corrections, and whether its results replicate.
