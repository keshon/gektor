# Education, learning resources, credentials, and careers

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ERIC](https://eric.ed.gov/) | Education research and indexed reports | Search the learner population and intervention or topic. Filter by publication type and inspect the study design. |
| [Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) | US occupations, training paths, and employment projections | Search occupation and verify the occupation code and data period. |
| School, accreditor, or licensing board | Current admissions, qualifications, accreditation, and licensing | Search the exact institution or credential and jurisdiction. Check the applicable intake or renewal year. |

Keep research claims, entry requirements, and labor statistics tied to their geography and dates.
