# Medicine research

Match the evidence to the question and the relevant country. For decisions
about care, check the applicable guideline and its date. Search with general
clinical terms and leave personal identifiers out of queries.

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/) | Patient-facing explanations of conditions, symptoms, tests, and medicines | Search the condition, symptom, or generic drug name. Open the topic or drug page and verify that it matches the question. |
| [NICE guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance) | UK clinical recommendations and their scope | Search for the condition plus `guideline`, then open the recommendations and check the publication or update date. For other countries, find the local guideline or health authority. |
| [DailyMed](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/) | US prescribing information, indications, contraindications, and warnings | Search the generic drug or brand and verify formulation, strength, and label date. For other countries, use the local regulator's product information. |
| [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) | Reviews and original studies for questions about evidence | Search condition plus intervention or exposure, then narrow by study type and date. Open the paper when details matter and check population, outcomes, and limitations. |

Use patient information for orientation, guidelines for recommendations,
labels for product-specific details, and studies for the evidence they
actually measured. Explain uncertainty when guidance differs or the evidence
covers a different population.
