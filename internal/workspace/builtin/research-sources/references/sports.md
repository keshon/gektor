# Sports, scores, rules, and records

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official league, federation, or event organizer | Results, fixtures, rules, standings, and records | Search the season, competition, team, and match date on the official site. Use the rules edition in effect for the event. |
| [Olympic results](https://www.olympics.com/en/olympic-games/olympic-results) | Olympic results by edition and sport | Search the Games year, sport, and event. Check the event result and whether it was later revised. |
| Team, club, or athlete announcements | Transfers, rosters, and official statements | Search the athlete or team on the club or league site, then verify when the announcement was made. |

Check the competition, time zone, and match status before reporting a schedule or live score.
