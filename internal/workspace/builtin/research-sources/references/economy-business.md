# Economics, companies, markets, and public statistics

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [World Bank Open Data](https://data.worldbank.org/) | Cross-country development and economic indicators | Search the indicator name and country. Read metadata for units, coverage, and revisions. |
| [Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/data/) | US employment, wages, and inflation series | Search the series name or ID and confirm frequency, seasonal adjustment, and units. |
| [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) | US public-company filings | Search the issuer name, ticker, or CIK. Filter by form, such as 10-K or 8-K, and compare filing dates. |

Compare like periods and definitions. Label currency, inflation basis, and reporting periods. Use the issuer or exchange for current figures.
