# Games, platforms, editions, and rules

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official developer, publisher, or platform store | Availability, system requirements, patch notes, and edition contents | Search the game, platform, and build number. Confirm page region and the date of the latest update. |
| [ESRB ratings search](https://www.esrb.org/search/) | ESRB ratings and content descriptors | Search the exact title and check the platform or edition. For other regions, use the local rating authority. |
| Official manual, rulebook, or game text | Rules, mechanics, and in-game lore | Search the specific term and edition. Use community wikis to locate the relevant passage, then check the original source when available. |

Keep platform versions, expansions, remasters, and house rules distinct.
