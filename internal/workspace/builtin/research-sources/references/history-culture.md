# History, archives, art, books, and music

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/collections/) | Digitized manuscripts, newspapers, photographs, and recordings | Search name, place, and date. Open collection notes and the item record for provenance. |
| [Smithsonian Open Access](https://www.si.edu/openaccess) | Museum objects, images, and collection metadata | Search the object or maker, then verify accession number, date, provenance, and rights on the record. |
| [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/search) | Identifying recordings, releases, and editions | Search artist and title, then compare country, label, barcode, and release date. |

Distinguish the date of an event, the creation date of an object, and the date it entered an archive. Use collection metadata to identify the item and the item itself for quotations.
