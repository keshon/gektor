# Maps, geography, transport, and travel

| Source | Use it for | How to search |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) | Location and map orientation | Search place name and locality. Confirm names, entrances, and coordinates against the venue or transport operator when precision matters. |
| [Wikivoyage](https://www.wikivoyage.org/) | Destination orientation and travel leads | Search destination plus country or region. Verify hours, fares, and schedules directly. |
| Official operator, embassy, or border authority | Current schedules, access rules, and entry requirements | Search the route and travel date on the operator site. For entry rules, check the destination authority for the traveler's citizenship and transit route. |

Use the exact travel date and local time. Check disruptions, closures, and entry rules close to departure.
