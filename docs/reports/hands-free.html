Hands-free conversation and interruption

.\agent.exe -profile profiles\chat-veyra-local.json -chat -tui -voice

Press Ctrl+L or choose Voice → Hands-free. Wait for Listening, then speak. English recognition produces a visible user turn and Chatterbox speaks the reply.

Silero VAD runs on CPU. Default onset confirmation is 200 ms, end silence 1100 ms, with 1.2 seconds of pre-roll. Speech → F4 Advanced tunes these timings and exposes an explicit energy fallback.

Confirmed speech cancels active generation/playback. The next turn waits for cancellation to complete. Continued speech during recognition retains earlier audio and delays submission. F8 switches to push-to-talk. Ctrl+L disables listening; Esc stops a response.

Opening settings pauses the microphone; enable Ctrl+L again afterward. A stalled stream is reported, with off/on reconnection. Capture, classifier and pending recognition are canceled on exit.
